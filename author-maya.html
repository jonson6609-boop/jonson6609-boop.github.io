About Maya

Maya writes most of the money and focus pieces on The Small Fix. Her approach is practical rather than theoretical — most of what she writes about comes from habits she's tested in her own budgeting, work routines, and daily decisions, including the ones that didn't work on the first attempt.

She is not a licensed financial advisor. Where a topic touches on decisions with real financial consequences, her posts note that plainly and point readers toward a qualified professional rather than presenting personal habits as formal advice.
