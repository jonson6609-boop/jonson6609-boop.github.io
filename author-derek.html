About Derek

Derek writes The Small Fix's Auto & Car Finance section, focused on the practical side of buying and owning a car: loan comparisons, dealer financing tactics, and the decisions that quietly cost buyers the most money. His goal with every piece is to explain the mechanics clearly enough that you can walk into a dealership or a loan application already knowing what to check.

He is not a licensed financial advisor or a dealership employee. Figures like typical APR ranges are general guidance, not a quote for your specific situation — always confirm current rates and terms directly with lenders before making a decision.
