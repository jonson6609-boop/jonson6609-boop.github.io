About Jonah

Jonah writes most of the home organization and wellbeing pieces on The Small Fix, usually after living with a system in his own house or routine long enough to know where it actually breaks down. He's upfront when something took months to work out, or when an early attempt failed before a slower version stuck.

He is not a medical professional or therapist. Posts that touch on sleep, stress, or mental health note this plainly and point readers toward a doctor or therapist for anything beyond general, low-stakes habits.
