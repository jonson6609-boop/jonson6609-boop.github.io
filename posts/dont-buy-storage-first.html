Don't buy storage until you've done this first

Published August 11, 2026 · 4 min read · By Jonah Pierce

Buying bins, baskets, and organizers feels productive, and it's often the first thing people reach for when a space feels cluttered. But storage bought before decluttering usually just neatly contains the same problem — it makes the mess look tidier without actually reducing it.

The order that actually works

Declutter the space completely first — decide what's staying and what's going, with everything out where you can see it. Only once you know exactly what's left should you measure what's remaining and buy storage sized specifically for that amount. Storage should follow the declutter, never precede it.

Why buying storage first backfires

An empty bin has a strange psychological pull — it feels like it needs to be filled, so it often gets filled with things that shouldn't have been kept in the first place. Storage bought before decluttering tends to expand to hold whatever clutter already existed, rather than being sized to what actually deserves to stay.

What "sized for what's left" actually looks like

Once you've decluttered a drawer or shelf, look at what's actually staying and measure roughly how much space it needs. If it fits without any new container at all, that's often the real answer — many spaces don't need bought storage, they need less stuff, full stop.

If you already own storage bought too early

Don't feel obligated to keep using it just because it was already purchased. Once you've properly decluttered a space, if the containers you already own are the wrong size or genuinely unnecessary, it's fine to donate or return them rather than forcing your newly decluttered space to fit containers that don't match it.

An organized version of too much stuff is still too much stuff. Storage should hold what's left after deciding, not decide for you.

Before your next storage purchase

If you're eyeing bins for a cluttered space, declutter that space fully first — even if it takes a few short sessions rather than one sitting. Whatever containers you actually need will become obvious once you can see exactly what's staying.
