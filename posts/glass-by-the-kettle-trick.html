The glass-by-the-kettle trick for drinking more water

Published August 13, 2026 · 3 min read · By Jonah Pierce

Most people don't forget to drink water on purpose. They just don't have a natural moment during the day that reminds them, so it quietly slips until they're already thirsty — which, by then, means they're already a bit behind. One small placement change gives an existing daily habit a built-in reminder, with nothing new to remember.

The trick: put a glass next to whatever you use every morning

The kettle, the coffee maker, the toaster — whatever you interact with daily without fail. Put a clean glass right beside it. While you wait for coffee or toast, that glass is right there, doing nothing else, at a moment you're already standing in the kitchen anyway.

Why this works better than a reminder app

Apps ask you to notice and respond to a notification — one more thing competing for attention. A glass sitting next to something you already touch every day needs no noticing at all; it's simply in the path of a habit you've already built. The reminder isn't a ping, it's a glass in your peripheral vision at exactly the right moment.

Extend it to other fixed daily moments

The same trick works anywhere you already have an unmissable daily cue: a glass by your desk for right when you sit down to work, one by the bathroom sink for right after brushing your teeth. Each cue only needs to catch you once a day — the habit adds up from a few small, reliable moments, not one big effort.

What to skip

Don't try to set up five new water stations at once — pick the one moment in your day that's most reliable, and start there. Once that one is automatic, which usually takes a couple of weeks, add a second if you want more.

You don't need to remember to drink more water. You need one glass sitting somewhere you were already going to be.

Set it up tomorrow morning

Put a glass by the kettle or coffee maker tonight, so it's there the moment you walk into the kitchen tomorrow. It costs nothing and takes ten seconds to set up — the only real step is remembering to actually drink from it once it's there.
